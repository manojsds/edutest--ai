# EduTest AI — Sales Pitch Guide for Outreach Team

## What is EduTest AI?

EduTest AI is an AI-powered test preparation platform for Indian competitive exam students (UPSC, JEE, NEET, SSC, Bank PO, KCET, etc.).

Students get unlimited, AI-generated practice tests with:
- Exam-specific question patterns (statement-based, assertion-reason, etc.)
- Real current affairs from live news sources
- Instant AI explanations for wrong answers
- Topic-wise performance tracking and weak area identification

1. **You share your referral code** with your students (WhatsApp, notice board, classroom)

## Talking Points for Calls / Visits

### Opening line:
> "We've built an AI test platform for [UPSC/JEE/NEET] students. We want to partner with your institute — you earn commission on every student, and it costs you nothing. Can I show you a 5-minute demo?"

### If they ask "What does it cost us?":
> "Nothing. Zero. We don't charge coaching centers. We charge students ₹270/month, and we pay you 10% of that automatically."

### If they ask "How is this different from TestBook/Unacademy?":
> "Those platforms compete with you. We work for you. Your students see your brand, not ours. And you earn money from it."

### If they ask "What if students don't like it?":
> "That's why we offer 1 week free. No commitment. If your students don't find it useful, there's nothing to lose."

### If they ask "How do we track earnings?":
> "You get a dashboard that shows every student, their subscription status, and your commission earned this month."
